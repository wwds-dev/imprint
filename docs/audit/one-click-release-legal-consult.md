## Facts from the code that change the framing of the brief
- **Quote graphics are not AI images.** `agents/manuscript/quote_graphics.py:74-118` draws the author's quote as text on a gradient with Pillow. No model makes them. AI only *chooses* the quotes, and the words are the author's own.
- **The on-device voice is macOS `say`.** The default voice is "Samantha" (`providers/voice/mock.py:69,76`), chosen through `shorts_generator.get_voice_provider` (`agents/manuscript/shorts_generator.py:27-32`). The owner chose it as the Shorts default (spec:145; D2 row 6, spec:190).
- **No AI marking is added to any output.**
  - Audiobook: the M4B metadata holds only the title and chapters (`services/narrator/converter.py:1015-1022`). The M4B merge replaces the source metadata (`-map_metadata 1`) and re-encodes to AAC (`:984-986`). Gemini audio is also re-encoded, from WAV to MP3 (`:920-922`).
  - Shorts: the MP4 gets no metadata (`shorts_generator.py:49-59`).
  - EPUB: carries only the title, author, a UUID and `lang="en"` (`agents/author/book_exporter.py:73-76`).
- **No voice-cloning code**, judged by a grep for `clone|ivc|voices/add` in `providers/`, `agents/audiobook`, `services/narrator`: no hits. But the ElevenLabs voice list returns every voice on the user's account (`providers/voice/elevenlabs.py:36-61`), which includes any cloned voice made on the ElevenLabs website.
- **Not new:** whole chapters already go to OpenAI, Gemini or ElevenLabs for Booth narration (app-context `## Models used`, rows OpenAI, Gemini and ElevenLabs).

## Proposed LAWYER triggers (proposals only; not written to `docs/legal/register.md`)
- **T1.** Whether Imprint is placed on the market or put into service, so that the owner is a provider under Art. 50(2) for synthetic audio and text. Also, which marking measures would meet it, and whether the 2 Dec 2026 date in Art. 111(4) applies. Evidence: `converter.py:984-986,1015-1022`, `shorts_generator.py:49-59`. The Commission guidelines C(2026) 5054 were not read.
- **T2.** Whether publishing Shorts narrated with macOS System Voices on TikTok or Instagram to promote a book is outside the licence (§2F, EA2005). Evidence: `mock.py:69,76`, spec:145.
- **T3.** Art. 50(4) deep-fake scope for synthetic narration voices, in particular cloned or real-person voices selectable from the ElevenLabs account (`elevenlabs.py:36-61`). This applies when slices 2 and 3 offer ElevenLabs in the kit.
- **T4** (conditional on slice 3). Sending whole-book text that may hold third-party or special-category personal data to non-EEA chat providers (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen) for quote sampling (spec:143). Covers transfer mechanism and DPIA. The rule was not verified against each provider's DPA in this consult.
