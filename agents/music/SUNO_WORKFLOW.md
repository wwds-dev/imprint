# Songs & Albums — Suno-assisted creation

Open Audio + Music → Music Artist Generator → Songs & Albums. Enter a title, track count and creative
brief. Choose the text provider/model above, then draft songs and Suno prompts.
This request uses Imprint's existing permission, budget and usage controls.

Edit the result and save it. Select an individual style prompt or lyrics block
and use Copy selected / all. Open Suno, create and review each song in your own
account, then download your chosen versions. Suno controls its generation costs
and download permissions; those charges are not tracked by Imprint.

Import audio copies those files into data/music_library without moving the
originals. Double-click to listen using your default player. Move up/down sets
the saved track order. Open album folder reveals the audio and album.json,
which records the title, brief, lyrics/prompts and sequence. Saved albums are
available in the dropdown when you reopen Imprint. Save edits before closing.

This workflow does not automatically generate Suno audio or publish music.
There is no Suno API key to configure. Review the rights attached to your Suno
downloads before distributing them. Song and album production still includes
your selection, editing, sequencing and listening review.
